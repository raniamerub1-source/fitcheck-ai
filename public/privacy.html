FitCheck AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version 1.0
Summary: FitCheck AI uses artificial intelligence (Google Gemini Vision API) to evaluate outfit photos and provide styling feedback. We process outfit images ephemerally in volatile memory. We do not store, sell, or use your photos to build biometric or behavioral profiles.

1. Overview & Data Controller

FitCheck AI ("we", "our", or "the App") provides an intelligent outfit styling and fashion critique assistant. This Privacy Policy explains how data is gathered, transmitted, and handled when you use FitCheck AI through the web application, Progressive Web App (PWA), or Android mobile application.

2. Data We Collect and Process

We only process data that you explicitly provide when requesting an outfit critique:

  • User-Uploaded Outfit Images: When you select or snap a photo of an outfit, the image file is encoded and transmitted to our secure backend worker over TLS (HTTPS).
  • Styling Preferences: Selected occasion (e.g. casual, formal, date night) and aesthetic vibe (e.g. minimalist, street, old money) chosen in the user interface.
  • Transient System Telemetry: Standard HTTP request headers (IP address, user agent) required for routing and abuse prevention.

3. Artificial Intelligence & Vision Processing

To generate fit scores, color balance analysis, silhouette proportions, and garment recommendations, outfit photos and selected styling preferences are submitted to Google Gemini Vision AI.

  • All data transmissions use secure encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3).
  • Image data is processed ephemerally in-memory solely for the duration of the styling inference.
  • FitCheck AI does not permanently store user photos in any database.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your photos or personal data to any third party.

4. Device Permissions

FitCheck AI accesses your device's photo gallery or camera exclusively through the standard operating system file picker when you tap the photo upload area. The application does not request or require background device camera permissions.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict security practices:

  • Server-side API keys and cloud secrets are protected in isolated runtime environments and never exposed to the client browser.
  • Client-side session caching uses your browser's local state and does not transmit data outside the app.
